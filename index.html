Hermes Workspace Readonly

Hermes Workspace Readonly is a personal integration used by its owner to connect Hermes with Google services through Google OAuth.

What it does

The integration uses only the Google permissions explicitly granted by the account owner. Google data is accessed only to carry out the owner's requested workflows, such as reading relevant Workspace information and supporting personal automation.

This application is intended for personal use and is not offered as a public service.
