Privacy Policy

Hermes Workspace Readonly is a personal application used by its owner to connect Hermes with Google services through OAuth.

Google user data

The application accesses only the Google user data covered by the OAuth permissions that the account owner explicitly grants. That data is used solely to perform workflows requested by the account owner.

Storage

OAuth credentials and configuration required for the connection may be stored by the owner on systems under the owner's control. The application is not designed to create a public database of Google user data.

Sharing and sale of data

Google user data accessed by this application is not sold. The application is not intended to disclose Google user data to third parties except where necessary to provide a user-requested function or where required by law.

Data retention and access revocation

The account owner can revoke the application's Google access at any time from the Google Account security settings. Locally stored credentials or application data can also be deleted by the owner.

Limited Use

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to the support email displayed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
